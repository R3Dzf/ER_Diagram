A QUICK TOUR

Find your flow

Grab the canvas and go.

Press and hold the left mouse button on empty space, then drag. No special tool or key is needed. Scroll to zoom around your pointer.

  1. Build with elements

    Add entities, relationships, and attributes. Select a parent before adding an attribute to connect it automatically.

  2. Make the connections

    Choose the Connect tool, then click two elements. Select the relationship to set each entity’s cardinality, participation, or role.

  3. Make it precise

    Use Properties for keys, weak entities, identifying relationships, and derived or multivalued attributes. Connect an attribute to its subattributes for a composite attribute.

  4. Keep it and share it

    Signed-in diagrams autosave to your account. Use Version history to restore a previous save. Export PNG, SVG, or vector PDF with A4/A3 and tiled pages. Save JSON for a portable backup.

You can also hold Space and drag, or use the middle mouse button, to pan from anywhere on the canvas.

ORDER, BUILT IN

Arrange your diagram

Arrange this view. The other notation keeps its own layout.

Every arrangement follows these rules

  • Verify a clear gap between every visible shape.
  • Align entities and tables on shared rows and columns.
  • Group attributes with their owner and put keys first.
  • Place relationships between participants when space allows.
  • Keep relationship lines and labels clear of shapes.
  • Keep disconnected groups separate.
  • Use the same order every time, regardless of creation order.

Layouts and drawing Undo are independent in Chen and Crow’s Foot. Names, attributes, and relationships stay shared.
